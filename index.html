📖 Lesson 1: What are Verbs?

Verbs are words that express action, occurrence, or state of being. They are essential to every sentence, as they tell us what someone or something is doing or what is happening.

🎭 Lesson 2: Kinds of Verbs

Action Verbs: Show what someone is doing (run, jump, write)
Linking Verbs: Connect subject to description (is, am, are, become, seem)
Helping Verbs: Assist the main verb (have, has, do, did, will, can)

📝 Lesson 4: Rules of Forming Verbs

Regular Verbs: Add -ed for past tense (walk → walked)
Irregular Verbs: Change form (go → went, eat → ate)
Progressive: Adding -ing (walk → walking)
Third Person Singular: Add -s or -es (he runs, she watches)

🤝 Lesson 5: Subject-Verb Agreement

Rule: The verb must agree with the subject in number and person.
Singular subject = singular verb (The cat is sleeping)
Plural subject = plural verb (The cats are sleeping)

✨ Lesson 6: Verbals and Verbal Phrases

Verbals: Verbs used as other parts of speech.
Gerunds: -ing form used as nouns (Running is fun)
Participles: -ing or -ed form used as adjectives (The running water)
Infinitives: "to" + verb used as noun, adjective, or adverb (I want to run)
